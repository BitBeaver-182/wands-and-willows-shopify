Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products, shipping, returns, and more.

Orders & Shipping

Standard shipping typically takes 5-7 business days within the continental United States. Express shipping (2-3 business days) and overnight options are available at checkout. International orders may take 10-21 business days depending on customs processing and destination country.

Yes! We ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on destination and package weight. Please note that customers are responsible for any customs duties, taxes, or fees imposed by their country.

Some items may have shipping restrictions due to size or licensing agreements. These restrictions will be noted on the product page.

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can also track your order by logging into your account and viewing your order history. If you checked out as a guest, use the tracking link provided in your shipping confirmation email.

All shipments are insured. If your package is lost in transit or arrives damaged, please contact our customer service team within 48 hours of the expected delivery date (for lost packages) or receipt (for damaged items). We'll work with the carrier to resolve the issue and send a replacement or issue a refund as needed.

Returns & Exchanges

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for most items. Products must be unused, in original packaging, and in resalable condition. Handmade wands, personalized items, and subscription boxes are final sale and cannot be returned unless defective.

To initiate a return, log into your account or contact our customer service team. You'll receive a prepaid return label (US orders only) and instructions.

Once we receive your return, please allow 5-7 business days for inspection and processing. Refunds are issued to the original payment method and may take an additional 3-5 business days to appear in your account, depending on your financial institution.

Yes! We're happy to help with exchanges. Contact our customer service team with your order number and the item you'd like to exchange. We'll provide instructions and, if the new item is in stock, we can often ship it before receiving your return to minimize wait time.

We sincerely apologize if you received an incorrect item. Please contact us immediately with photos of the item received and your order number. We'll send the correct item via expedited shipping at no charge and provide a prepaid return label for the incorrect product.

Products & Care

Yes! All Harry Potter merchandise in our collection is officially licensed by Warner Bros. and comes with authenticity certificates. We work directly with licensed manufacturers to ensure quality and legitimacy. Products clearly marked as "inspired by" or "handmade" are artisan creations that celebrate magical themes without using licensed imagery.

Our handmade wands are crafted from real wood and sealed with protective finishes. To maintain their beauty:

  • Keep away from extreme temperatures and direct sunlight
  • Avoid prolonged exposure to moisture
  • Clean gently with a soft, dry cloth
  • Store in a cool, dry place when not displayed
  • Handle with care to prevent drops or impacts

Yes! Our apothecary candles are made with 100% natural soy wax, cotton wicks, and premium fragrance oils. They're vegan, cruelty-free, and free from parabens, phthalates, and petroleum. Each candle is hand-poured in small batches to ensure quality and consistency.

Our jewelry collection features a variety of materials including sterling silver, gold-plated brass, stainless steel, and hypoallergenic alloys. Each product listing specifies the exact materials used. For those with sensitive skin, we recommend our sterling silver or stainless steel options.

Most items are restocked regularly, though handmade and limited edition pieces may not return. You can sign up for restock notifications on any product page by clicking "Notify When Available." We'll email you as soon as the item is back in stock.

Subscription Boxes

Each monthly box contains 3-5 curated items valued at $75-100+, including:

  • Exclusive merchandise not available in our regular shop
  • Handcrafted artisan goods
  • Collectible items and prints
  • Apothecary products (candles, bath items, etc.)
  • A themed letter from our curator explaining the month's theme

Yes! When you subscribe, you'll select your preferred House (Gryffindor, Hufflepuff, Ravenclaw, or Slytherin). While not every item will be House-specific, we'll include at least one personalized piece in your House colors each month. You can update your House preference in your account settings.

Boxes ship during the first week of each month. If you subscribe mid-month, you'll receive the current month's box if it hasn't shipped yet; otherwise, you'll receive the following month's box. Renewal billing occurs on the 1st of each month.

You can cancel or pause your subscription anytime from your account dashboard. To avoid being charged for the next month, please make changes before the 25th of the current month. There are no cancellation fees, and you can reactivate a paused subscription whenever you'd like.
