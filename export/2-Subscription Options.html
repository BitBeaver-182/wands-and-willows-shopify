Frequently Asked Questions

When will my first box arrive?

If you subscribe before the 10th of the month, you'll receive your first box by the 15th of that same month. Subscriptions after the 10th will receive their first box the following month.

Can I cancel or pause my subscription?

Absolutely! You can cancel or pause your subscription at any time from your account dashboard. There are no cancellation fees or penalties.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We ship to most countries worldwide. International shipping rates vary by location and are calculated at checkout. Delivery times are typically 7-14 business days.

Can I gift a subscription?

Yes! Gift subscriptions are available for 3, 6, or 12 months. You'll receive a beautiful gift card to present to the recipient, and they can choose their preferred House theme.

What if I don't like an item in my box?

While we can't accept returns on subscription boxes, we stand by our quality. If you receive a damaged item or have concerns, please contact our customer service team within 7 days for a resolution.

Can I switch my House preference?

Yes! You can update your House preference in your account settings before the 5th of each month, and your next box will reflect the change.
