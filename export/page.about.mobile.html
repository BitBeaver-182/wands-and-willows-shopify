Our Story

A journey from a single handcrafted wand to a thriving community of magic-makers and dreamers.

The Beginning

Where Magic Met Craftsmanship

It started in a small apartment in Edinburgh, where I spent countless evenings trying to recreate the perfect wand from my favorite stories. What began as a personal obsession soon became something much larger—a mission to bring authentic, meaningful magical artifacts into the hands of fellow enthusiasts.

I was frustrated by mass-produced replicas that lacked soul. I wanted pieces that felt real, that carried weight and history, that made you believe in magic again. So I taught myself traditional woodworking, studied historical craftsmanship, and poured my heart into every detail.

That first wand took me three weeks to complete. When I finally held it in my hands, I knew I had found my calling.

"Magic isn't just found in books; it's woven into the very fabric of our daily lives."

— Eleanor Vance, Founder

Our Journey

2019

The First Wand

Eleanor crafts her first wand in her Edinburgh apartment. Friends and family are amazed by the quality and detail. Word spreads through local fan communities, and the first orders begin to arrive.

2020

Wand & Willow is Born

The brand officially launches with a small collection of handcrafted wands and apothecary candles. An Etsy shop gains traction, and Eleanor hires her first assistant to help with production. The community begins to form.

2021

Expanding the Collection

Jewelry, stationery, and home goods are added to the catalog. Wand & Willow secures official licensing partnerships with major studios. The team grows to five artisans, each specializing in different crafts.

2022

The Monthly Dose of Magic

The subscription box launches, offering curated monthly parcels of exclusive items. It becomes an instant hit, with over 2,000 subscribers in the first month. A dedicated workshop space is established in Glasgow.

2023

Building the Common Room

The online community flourishes with forums, virtual events, and meetups. Wand & Willow hosts its first in-person gathering in London, bringing together hundreds of fans. The team expands to 15 members.

2024

A New Chapter

Wand & Willow expands internationally, shipping to over 40 countries. New product lines inspired by Wicked and other magical worlds are introduced. The brand continues to grow while staying true to its artisanal roots.
